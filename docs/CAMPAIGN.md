# Campaign scenarios

Campaign metadata and objective rules live in `src/game/campaign.js`. Mission indices are zero based; existing IDs and indices remain stable for saves, ranked replays, and progress. Branch missions append to the catalog but appear between Black Shard and Red Ledger in the selector; bonus sequels append after the campaign route.

Completing Black Shard opens both routes. Winning either route at the chosen difficulty opens Red Ledger; the other route stays replayable. The first route completed is recorded as the commander's chosen path. Existing saves that had already reached Red Ledger retain access, including saves whose old unlock counter was exactly four without a medal ledger. Branch victories do not advance the old numeric counter to index 14 or 15. The two orders on each branch mission brought the campaign-wide total to 30; Iron Reclaimed raised it to 32, and Counterbattery brings it to 34. Carryover Intel into a branch uses verified Black Shard orders; into Red Ledger it uses orders from a completed branch, or Black Shard for a pre-fork profile without a verified branch.

The bonus sequel chain continues at the same difficulty: After the Dawn unlocks Iron Reclaimed, and a local medal or verified Iron Reclaimed victory unlocks Counterbattery for practice. Ranked Counterbattery tickets require a same-difficulty verified Iron Reclaimed victory; archived Iron Reclaimed replays meeting its version-73 minimum remain eligible. Counterbattery is bonus content and does not advance ordinary campaign progress. Its carryover Field Orders and veteran source come from Iron Reclaimed.

Version 54 adds a defended-economy raid to First Harvest. A warning marks the southern pass, where a Vesper raiding column targets the player Harvester. Repel the raid and complete the existing second-refinery and 2,200-credit objective. Replay versions before 54 keep the economy-only victory rule.

Version 55 changes Iron Current's final wave into a direct freight-relay assault. The reserve Guardians wait near the yard for player orders, while a Vesper tank and rocket escort press the crossing. From that wave's arrival, a 20-second uncontested enemy relay hold severs the line. Both attackers must be cleared before the final 12-second hold advances. Older replays and markerless saves retain their previous Iron Current rules.

Local ordinary-mission unlock progress is now recorded separately for Easy, Normal, and Hard. The selector's cleared counts and completed labels use the selected difficulty, as do medals and branch records. A legacy save with only the former shared unlock counter seeds all three ledgers once, preserving its previously available missions; subsequent victories advance only the difficulty played. Existing per-difficulty medal records supplement that migration when they show further ordinary-mission completion. The old counter remains written for compatibility but no longer drives the current selector. The fixed-force operations at indices 5, 12, and 13 present Field Support immediately and hide production, research, and base-only support cards while retaining command abilities, relay protocols, and the unit order dock.

Red Ledger pays off the first route in gameplay. **Ghost Channel** reveals the Aegis payroll courier and nearby escort for the opening 20 seconds, and a new sortie must issue an Attack order against the courier and destroy it before its refinery and treasury objective can complete. **Iron Current** provides an extra Engineer and a 700-credit freight cache at the eastern crossing; a new sortie must recover that named cache with a player Engineer through the ordinary wreck-recovery order. Both routes still require a second powered refinery and 4,800 credits before the six-minute deadline. A local sortie uses the first branch recorded at that difficulty. A ranked ticket derives the first verified branch victory on the server, locks the payoff into the run, and rejects a client-selected payoff. Pre-fork saves and replay versions 1–22 remain neutral; replay versions 23–45 retain their existing route assets and economy-only objective. New version 46 replays use the route-specific objective. Previously saved unversioned battles without the new campaign objective marker also keep their economy-only rule; saved games normalize a missing or invalid route ID to `none`.

`createCampaignGame(index, difficulty)` returns a regular `Game` initialized with campaign-specific faction, forces, briefing, and objective state. `updateCampaign(game, index, dt)` is called after `Game.update(dt)`; it returns `true` only on the completion frame and sets the normal `status`, `winner`, and campaign completion events. The game engine's default total-elimination victory is suppressed until the authored objective is met. The engine's ordinary defeat remains authoritative, with explicit mission losses for deadlines, the escort, and objective-critical structures.

Campaign briefings offer four selectable doctrines on every sortie, including fixed-force operations. The stable IDs and effects are:

| ID | Briefing name | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `standard` | Standard | Standard unit attributes. |
| `rapid` | Rapid | Player campaign units move 12% faster. |
| `reinforced` | Reinforced | Player campaign units have 12% more maximum health. |
| `precision` | Precision | Player campaign unit weapons deal 10% more damage. |

These are per-sortie choices, not earned unlocks. Effects apply only to units owned by the player in campaign mode; enemy units and skirmish units do not receive them. `createCampaignGame(index, difficulty, seed, doctrineId)` accepts the selected doctrine as its fourth argument. The browser stores the selection separately as its campaign briefing preference and preselects it on later briefings. The active campaign game serializes its doctrine, so save/load and replay playback retain the sortie's choice. Older saves or replay envelopes without a doctrine default to `standard`.

Each campaign operation also offers two optional Field Orders in addition to **Primary objective only**. Their authored objective and reward text live in `CAMPAIGN_FIELD_ORDERS`; the briefing presents them as a keyboard-operable radio group. Field Order IDs are per-mission and are validated against the selected operation. The browser remembers the last choice, but only preselects it when that ID is available for the current mission; otherwise it selects `none`. `createCampaignGame(index, difficulty, seed, doctrineId, fieldOrderId)` accepts the selected order as its fifth argument. An active order is tracked by campaign state, exposed through `getCampaignFieldOrderView(game)`, and serialized with the game. Saves, replay envelopes, and run tickets retain the locked ID. Legacy saves and replays without an ID use `none`. The in-mission tracker reports its current status and progress, and the debrief records whether the order was completed, failed, or not selected.

Terrain is painted when a new campaign game is created, using an index-specific seed for the tile details and a fixed set of rock walls, water, crystal fields, base footprints, and forces. Each map has a traversable route to its objective. The normal skirmish terrain generator and AI are unchanged. Relay sites are placed again after the authored terrain and structures, so relays remain on open ground.

The original twelve operations retain stable IDs and order for existing saves; bonus sequels follow the campaign route. The medal calculation grants one star for completion, one for preserving the mission's key asset, and one for meeting its authored par time while preserving that asset. The bonus operation uses the engineer as its key asset. The selector stores a best medal separately for each difficulty.

Campaign state is stored directly on `Game` (`campaignMission`, `campaignState`, `campaignComplete`, `campaignDoctrineId`, `campaignFieldOrderId`, and `campaignRoutePayoffId`), so `Game.serialize()` and `Game.deserialize()` preserve the active mission, doctrine, optional order, and route payoff without a campaign-specific save format. Existing saves retain their saved terrain and entities; only new games use the authored layouts. Replaying calls `createCampaignGame` again with the same index, difficulty, seed, doctrine, Field Order, and route payoff.

In the finale, the named enemy command yard carries a saved `campaignShielded` flag until the relay hold finishes. Damage is ignored while the shield is active, including any XP or salvage that attack would have awarded. The phase transition clears the flag and normal combat resumes.

The bonus rescue stores its engineer ID, relay ID, extraction point, timer, and phase in `campaignState`. Relay hold progress requires uncontested player ownership and the engineer on foot within range. On Hard, completing the hold starts a saved five-second patrol warning; a Vesper buggy and rifle then move toward the east-bank landing. The warning and patrol each fire once, including after save/load. Version 4 local replays retain the original encounter without this patrol. The operation is deterministic and uses the ordinary transport orders, save format, and replay verifier.

In current rules, a player-owned uncontested relay can directly select Shelter, Overdrive, or Logistics from Support. Shelter protects nearby forces from ion surges and lets Aegis infantry recover. Overdrive adds 0.45 command energy per second and 15% nearby ground-unit reload speed. Logistics adds 10% of accepted Harvester cargo at refinery unload per secure Logistics relay, up to 30% and within credit storage capacity. Logistics earns nothing while harvesting is idle or the relay is contested. Both alternatives trade away shelter; each switch locks the relay for 12 simulation seconds. Campaign saves retain the choice. Replays through version 48 retain the original two protocols, and replays from version 6 or earlier keep their original relay behavior. Version 50 Stormglass Bloom rules apply only to skirmish and multiplayer; campaign storms retain the existing crystal rules.

## Iron Reclaimed — rules 73

Complete After the Dawn at the selected difficulty to unlock this second bonus operation. Existing mission IDs and indices remain unchanged. Local medals unlock unranked practice; ranked tickets require that profile’s verified rescue victory. Earned Survivor Attachments, Carryover Intel, and supply packages retain their usual rules. No construction or replacement units are available on the map.

The north pass faces rocket infantry and the central patrol; the southern crossing reaches a separate eastern marker while avoiding that central approach. Each route reveals its approaching patrol and gives a five-second warning. The two optional Field Orders require a direct Attack order against the marked buggy or rocket team and award a refit to surviving units: +25% maximum health and recovery equal to 35% of their former maximum. Ordinary cash salvage of the mission hull fails the objective immediately; use **RECOMMISSION WRECK**, or **RESTORE** on a phone. The original Engineer and the actual restored Guardian must survive; a different vehicle cannot replace either objective identity.

`node scripts/iron-reclaimed-public-playtest.js` runs both Normal routes and the Hard southern route using five ordinary commands on the production 30 Hz clock. Seed 41073 completed north in 151.967 seconds and south in 135.233 seconds without optional advantages. These are bounded command policies, not a win-rate or human difficulty measurement. The full southern recording is also compared with deterministic playback in `tests/iron_reclaimed_replay.test.js`.


## Operation: Counterbattery — rules 84

Counterbattery is campaign index 16 and a bonus sequel to Iron Reclaimed. Local completion at a difficulty unlocks practice at that difficulty; ranked access requires a verified Iron Reclaimed victory at the same difficulty. The mission requires destroying both Vesper Siege Crawlers within 210 seconds while keeping the Command Yard alive. The original Forward Radar is threatened by the northern battery and the Forward Refinery by the southern one. Its Field Orders are **Cut the Siege Supply** (destroy the enemy Harvester with a direct Attack order) and **Claim the East Relay**. The original Forward Radar remains a Siege Crawler target, not a Field Order objective. Iron Reclaimed supplies its carryover Field Orders and veteran source. The art prompt and provenance are recorded in [COUNTERBATTERY_ART.md](COUNTERBATTERY_ART.md).

New solo replays use version 84. Only the Counterbattery campaign comparison board advances to floor 84; earlier campaign and skirmish floors remain 83, and ranked multiplayer remains Season 83. Existing verified results, private history, and campaign progression are retained.

The third medal requires a separate 165-second par. North/South battery buttons in the mission tracker center the camera on known objective intelligence without revealing hidden movement or HP; silenced batteries disable their button. `node scripts/counterbattery-public-playtest.js` demonstrates one Normal seed-24 air route: six paid gunship queues, victory at 173.4 seconds with the Yard intact, and exact replay. Its untouched baseline loses at 210 seconds. This is one bounded solution; ground-route balance needs further play.
