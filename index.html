      FIELD MANUAL // 01

      COMMAND THE FRONTIER

      Choose a field topic to read its orders. Open several to compare tactics.

      01 / ECONOMY+

      Build a refinery. Harvesters collect glowing crystal and return it for credits. Expand to more fields to fund your army. Silo capacity prevents waste. Aegis extracts extra crystal from fields near a powered friendly refinery. Vesper combat units recover salvage credits when they destroy completed enemy structures. Most destroyed ground vehicles leave salvage for two minutes: select an Engineer and right click a visible wreck, or use the Engineer task button on touch, then protect the Engineer through its four second recovery. Either commander can claim it.

      02 / BASE & RESEARCH+

      Place structures near your existing base. Power plants keep production and radar online. Build barracks, factories and a Research Center to unlock stronger units. A powered Research Center lets you choose one permanent focus from the Research tab: Logistics speeds harvesting, Siege increases structure damage, or Signal restores command energy and ability cooldowns faster. Research costs credits and takes time; losing power pauses progress. After a focus finishes, choose one Tactical Package in Research for 700 credits and 38 seconds. Breach Window, Interdiction Pulse, or Rally Signal then appears in Support as a targetable command with its own energy cost and cooldown. Defense Walls block ground approaches. Adjacent friendly ground units behind a wall take 28% less direct ballistic and cannon damage; splash and flanking fire bypass this cover. Walls can be repaired or sold, but cannot keep a base alive. After a War Factory, build a powered Field Workshop near the front to repair friendly ground vehicles for credits.

      03 / COMMAND+

      Left click selects. Drag to select a squad. Selecting one armed unit or powered defense shows its firing radius on the battlefield. Right click moves, attacks a target, or sends a harvester to crystal. Select units and press ROUTE at the bottom of the battlefield to tap successive waypoints or visible enemies; ATTACK or MOVE changes the planned order type, and ROUTE or Escape ends planning. Holding Shift while right-clicking also queues orders. On touch screens, tap to select, drag the battlefield to pan, or hold briefly then drag to box-select a squad; use command buttons to target orders. A loaded Harvester can Return Cargo early with J; each Refinery unloads one Harvester at a time. Alt + left click or M then click force moves without engaging; N cycles through your units, starting with idle combat units, and centers the view. Attack Move fights through a destination; Guard holds an anchor, Patrol loops to a clicked endpoint, Follow escorts an ally, and Scatter spreads a threatened squad. Force Fire targets ground, including empty terrain. Cycle combat stances with T: Aggressive engages freely, Defensive stays near its post, and Hold Fire waits for an explicit attack order. Armed light and heavy ground vehicles take 15% less damage from attacks arriving within 60° of their front and 20% more from behind; side hits are unchanged. Turn armor toward incoming fire and split your force to flank. A selected vehicle marks its protected front in cyan and exposed rear in amber. Splash checks each vehicle separately. Vesper Specter Tanks suppress a ground unit's weapon on their first concealed strike. Select one Guardian or Specter tank and use its field action, or press E: Brace protects nearby ground armor for seven seconds; Ghost Run requires a move order and speeds that flank while its weapon is silent for six seconds. Both recover in 30 seconds. Use the sidebar to construct and train. When a friendly force takes damage, the live-feed badge at the foot of the battlefield shows its sector; select the badge to center on it.

      04 / RELAYS & STORMS+

      Hold Resonance Relays with nearby forces to gain command energy. Select RELAYS in the command header to choose a protocol directly for each uncontested relay you own. Shelter protects nearby forces from ion storms; a secure Aegis Shelter relay also restores nearby friendly infantry at 1 HP per second. While secure, Overdrive adds +0.45 command energy per second and lets friendly ground units within 2.3 tiles reload 15% faster. Logistics adds 10% of accepted Harvester cargo for each secure relay set to it when cargo unloads at a Refinery, up to 30% and only within credit storage capacity. It earns nothing without deliveries or while contested. Overdrive and Logistics trade away that relay’s storm shelter and Aegis recovery; Logistics also trades away Overdrive’s reload and energy bonus. Each switch locks for 12 seconds. Contested and unowned relays cannot switch protocols. Older battle replays retain their two original protocols. Storm surges enrich crystal fields, conceal threats, and damage exposed units. In skirmish and multiplayer, each surge also opens a public 90-second Stormglass Bloom around an enriched crystal patch. Click STORMGLASS in the command header to locate it. Select a Harvester with at least one armed ground escort per Harvester; while the Bloom is active, HARVEST becomes BLOOM. Choose it or press Shift+H to send the group to reachable charged crystal. Loaded Harvesters return to a Refinery when the Bloom closes. Press H for ordinary harvesting or give a direct order to override the expedition. Crystal gathered there earns 35% extra when delivered to a Refinery, within credit storage capacity; the premium remains on cargo after the Bloom closes. Guard the route through the storm. Use Tactical Scan, Overcharge, and Shield Pulse from Support. During an ion warning or surge, Relay Stormcall can pull the storm toward a visible point near one of your relays. Breach Window marks a visible area where friendly ground weapons hit structures 45% harder for 12 seconds; Interdiction suppresses visible enemy mobile weapons for three seconds; Rally heals and redirects nearby armed ground units. Each is exclusive to its researched package.

      05 / VICTORY+

      Scout through the fog, secure resource fields, and complete your operation objective. Choose Elimination to destroy the rival's combat forces, command, and active industry, or Relay Dominion to hold two relays for 90 seconds (120 seconds on Cadet). In solo Elimination, amber last-seen contacts in Objects and on powered radar mark old hostile sightings; scout those sectors to confirm. Keep your command base alive. After a complete solo battle, watch its local replay from the debrief or title screen.

      06 / VETERANCY+

      Combat units earn experience by damaging and defeating enemies. Veterans gain 5% damage and maximum health; Elite units gain 10%. When an armed ground unit reaches Elite, select it and choose one permanent field modification from the selection card: Bulwark reduces incoming damage by 15% after armor and cover, while Rangefinder adds one tile to weapon range. The selected unit's firing-radius guide reflects Rangefinder. The choice lasts for that battle and survives a saved battle.

      07 / TRANSPORT+

      Train an Armored Carrier at a War Factory for three protected infantry seats, or an Airlift Dropship at a Helipad for four seats and fast routes over water and cliffs. Select infantry and right click a friendly carrier, or press L and target it. Select a loaded carrier, press U, and choose clear ground to deploy. Destroyed carriers may eject survivors nearby with heavy damage.

      08 / BATTLE RECORDS+

      Complete a solo operation to keep its latest replay on this device. The debrief lets you watch it with pause and speed controls. When online, Commander Records verifies a complete battle from its recorded orders before ranking a victory; loading a mid-battle save starts an unranked session. Ranked campaign missions unlock from this profile’s verified victories at the selected difficulty. Local medals still allow offline practice. A living armed Survivor Attachment from a completed mission can join the next operation, retaining its earned rank and Elite modification.

      09 / SQUAD GROUPS+

      Organize forces for fast flanks and defense. On a narrow screen, select units, tap + beside a squad number to save them, then tap that number to recall the group. The count shows surviving members and the bright border marks the current selection. Keyboard players can assign with Ctrl + 1…9 and recall with 1…9; Shift + number also centers the camera on a recalled group.
